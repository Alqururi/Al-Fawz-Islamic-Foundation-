

AL FAWZ ISLAMIC FOUNDATION – OFFICIAL APP

Empowering the Ummah: Dawah, Education and Charity.

Welcome to the official mobile application of Al Fawz Islamic Foundation, a platform dedicated to promoting Islamic knowledge, spiritual development, educational excellence and humanitarian service.

Al Fawz Islamic Foundation is committed to empowering individuals and communities through authentic Islamic teachings, accessible educational opportunities, charitable initiatives and programmes that contribute to the moral, intellectual and spiritual development of the Ummah.

Through this application, users can stay connected with the Foundation, access Islamic educational resources, participate in programmes and competitions, discover scholarship opportunities, register for events, follow important announcements and contribute to charitable projects.

Our Key Activities:

- Dawah and Islamic Enlightenment.
- Qur'an Education, Memorisation and Competitions.
- Islamic Webinars, Lectures and Conferences.
- Educational Scholarships and Sponsorships.
- Youth Development and Empowerment.
- Academic and Intellectual Competitions.
- Humanitarian and Charitable Projects.
- Community Development Initiatives.

Our Vision: To contribute to the development of a knowledgeable, morally upright, spiritually conscious and socially responsible Ummah.

Our Mission: To empower individuals and communities through Dawah, quality education and sustainable charitable initiatives, guided by the teachings of the Qur'an and Sunnah.

Join us in building a knowledgeable, compassionate and empowered Ummah.
